# Code of Conduct

Gralloch welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Everyone participating in this project is expected to help maintain a respectful,
constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Discuss the code and evidence; avoid personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect people's privacy and their decision to leave a discussion.
- Keep research discussions and shared examples within the project's isolation
  and sample-handling rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, sustained personal attacks, and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Sharing harmful files to target participants or pressuring them to disable
  protections or execute an untrusted sample.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents
the project in public.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact channel they
have published, if one is available. Include relevant links, the behavior involved,
and any immediate safety concern. Share only the information needed to understand
the report.

If no private channel is available, you may open an issue asking for a private
way to report a conduct concern. Do not include incident details, personal
information, or the identities of affected people in that public request.
You can also use the hosting platform's reporting tools for abuse on that platform.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and limit disclosure to what is
needed to investigate and respond. They should not promise absolute confidentiality
or a particular outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask participants to stop
a behavior, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
The response should reflect the severity, context, and any repeated behavior.

Where practical, maintainers should explain the reason for an action privately.
A participant may request reconsideration through an available private contact
channel and provide additional context. Anyone handling a report should disclose
relevant conflicts of interest and step aside when another maintainer can handle it.
